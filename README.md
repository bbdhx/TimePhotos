# TimePhotos

A photo captures a moment. Time and place give it a story.

TimePhotos brings your photography together through time, location, and personal expression. Capture new moments, import photos from your library, explore where they were taken, and turn them into beautifully designed Photo Cards.

More than a photo collection, TimePhotos is a place to keep, explore, and present the moments that matter.

MAKE EVERY PHOTO YOUR OWN

Turn your photos into personal Photo Cards and give every moment its own style.

• Add custom signatures and captions to make each card personal.
• Choose from different card styles and colors.
• Customize the look and feel of your cards.
• Adjust how your photos are displayed to create the perfect composition.

Every detail is yours to shape. Give each card its own character, or bring a consistent style to your entire collection.

EXPLORE YOUR PHOTOS ON THE MAP

Every photo has a place. Discover yours.

See your photos on an interactive map and explore the places behind your memories.

• View photos at the places where they were taken.
• Easily explore areas with multiple photos.
• Discover individual photos and their details as you explore the map.
• Rediscover familiar places and faraway destinations through your photography.

CAPTURE THE MOMENT

Sometimes the best memories begin with a simple tap.

Use the built-in camera to capture moments without leaving TimePhotos. Your photos are connected with their shooting time and location, helping you remember not just what you saw, but where and when you were there.

Already have a photo collection? Import photos from your library and bring your existing memories into TimePhotos.

KEEP AND SHARE YOUR CREATIONS

Your Photo Cards are kept in your TimePhotos collection, ready to revisit whenever you like.

Want to take a card beyond the app? Export it as an image and save it to your Photos library. Keep your favorite designs, share them, or simply enjoy them as little pieces of your photographic story.

A MOMENT, A PLACE, A STORY.

TimePhotos is built around a simple idea: photographs are more than images. They are little records of where we were, when we were there, and what we chose to remember.

Give every photo its place.
Give every moment its own card.
